Player's Manual

Controls: Arrow keys to move. Enter to start, P to pause, Esc to menu.

Orbital Dash: Move with mouse. Catch colored orbs (+1/+2/+3/+5), gold (+10) gives shield. Emerald grants 6s shield + 2x points. Avoid red triangles.

Bubble Pop: Click groups of 2+ matching colors. Bubbles sink to bottom. Lose if bottom row fills.

Candy Fall: Arrows move, Up rotates, Space hard-drops. Clear lines for bonus points.

Snake: Arrow keys steer. Apples = points (Easy:1/Med:3/Hard:6). Gold bonus after 4-6 apples (Easy:10/Med:20/Hard:35). Maps rotate each run.

Themes: Light, Dark, Neon. Change in menu or use Theme button in-game.

Scoreboard: Top 10 per game stored locally in this browser.
